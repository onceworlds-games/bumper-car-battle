# Bumper Car Battle

Bump everyone off the rink and be the last car standing. Boost into the other cars, knock them into the water, and stay on the floor as it shrinks.

Play it: https://onceworlds.com/play/bumper-car-battle

Drive with the stick (or W A S D / arrows) and press Boost (Space) to ram. Up to 10 cars in a match; bots fill the empty seats.

Plain ES modules, Canvas 2D and Web Audio, no build. `npm test` runs the physics, rules and a full bot match. MIT licensed.
